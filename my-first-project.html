Delicious Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Mix all dry ingredients together.
  2. Whisk in milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Heat a pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour batter and cook until bubbles form. Flip and cook the other side.
  5. Serve hot with syrup or fruits!
